Impact on the Target

Impact Category: The type or domain of impact caused by an action or event. This categorizes how the impact affects an organization or system.

Impacted Refs:  A list of STIX objects that were affected by the impact (such as assets, systems, data, or entities). These references identify what the impact directly influenced or damaged.

Recoverability: An assessment of how difficult or resource-intensive it is to restore affected assets or return operations to normal after the impact.
