Common Controls: A shared safety measure or security practice that is used across different systems, departments, or organizations to protect against threats.

Common Weaknesses:  A “weakness” is a condition in software, firmware, or hardware that, under certain circumstances, could contribute to a vulnerability within a system.

Common Vulnerabilities: Weak spots or flaws found in software or systems that make them easy targets for hackers or malicious software.
